FocusFlow AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 July 2026 · Last updated: 22 July 2026

FocusFlow AI (“FocusFlow”, “we”, “the app”) is a productivity app for planning and running focus sessions on Apple devices. This policy describes how FocusFlow handles information.

No data collection

FocusFlow does not collect personal data. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting vendor, no account system, and no server backend operated by us. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer user data to third parties because we do not receive it.

Fully on-device processing

All processing happens fully on-device on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac:

  • Calendar access (when you grant it) is used only locally to find free windows for focus suggestions. Calendar contents never leave the device through FocusFlow.
  • On-device AI suggestions use Apple’s FoundationModels framework (SystemLanguageModel) entirely on your device. Prompts, calendar-derived context, and model output stay on device. When Apple Intelligence is unavailable, a deterministic rule-based engine runs locally instead.
  • Session history, stats, settings, and subscription status are stored in local app storage (and, only if you later enable iCloud sync via a future build flag and portal configuration, in your personal iCloud container — still not on any FocusFlow server).
  • Live Activities, widgets, notifications, and Focus-status checks run through Apple system APIs on the same device.

Permissions

FocusFlow may ask for Calendar, Notifications, and Focus status. Each is optional; declining any of them degrades only the related feature. You can revoke permissions at any time in system Settings.

Purchases

Optional FocusFlow Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit / App Store billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchase processing.

Children

FocusFlow is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from children. There is no sign-up flow and no data collection surface.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will carry a new “Last updated” date. Material changes that affect App Store privacy labels will be reflected in App Store Connect before the corresponding app version ships.
